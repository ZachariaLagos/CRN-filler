# NU Swap

Press a button once: 10 seconds later it checks **one** Northeastern section for
an open seat. If there is one, it swaps it in for a course you already have, using
Banner's **Conditional Add and Drop**, and submits. It never checks again on its
own; press the button for another check.

## What one press does
1. Waits 10 seconds (countdown on the page).
2. Find Classes → Advanced Search → Subject + College + Campus → Search, once.
3. Finds your new CRN in the results and reads its seats.
4. Full → stops, nothing changed.
5. Open → clicks **Add**, sets your current course to **Web Drop** in the Summary,
   ticks **Conditional Add and Drop**, and clicks **Submit**.

**Credit hours:** after the swap you must still meet your minimum credit hours
(for example, full-time status). If dropping the old course would put you below
the minimum, Banner can reject the swap. Check your hours before you press the
button, especially if you're an international student.

Safety: it does nothing unless the course to drop is currently registered, and it
never submits without Conditional Add and Drop ticked, so the drop only happens if
the add succeeds.

## Use
Open **Register for Classes** (Find Classes / Enter CRNs tabs), open the popup,
enter the new CRN, the CRN to drop, and the filters, then press
**Check in 10 s & swap**. Turn off NU CRN Filler's schedule while using this.
