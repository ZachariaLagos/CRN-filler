Swap

Press once: 10 s later it checks the new section one time. If a seat is open, it adds it and drops your old course with Conditional Add and Drop, then submits. Press again for another check.

The drop only happens if the new course is added.

Credit check: after the swap you must still meet your minimum credit hours (for example, full-time). If the drop would put you below the minimum, Banner can reject the whole swap.
