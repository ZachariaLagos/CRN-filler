CRN Filler

Searches Find Classes by Subject, College and Campus, finds your CRNs, and adds them.

The courses you want, one per line. The first one is tried through the Enter CRNs tab first when that tab exists.

Tried in order, only for a main course that fails to register.

Typed into Advanced Search exactly as Banner’s dropdowns word them. Leave College or Campus blank to skip that filter.

Register when my window opens

At this time it clicks Continue, then: 1) if there’s an Enter CRNs tab, types your first main CRN, Add to Summary, Submit; 2) if there’s a Plans tab with a plan, clicks Add All on Plan 1 and Submit; 3) fills anything still missing by searching Subject / College / Campus through your CRNs in order. It stops once you have enough.

On the term page, pick your term but don’t click Continue. The extension clicks it at this time.
